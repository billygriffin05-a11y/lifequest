# LIFEQUEST roadmap — next 30 features

Built in batches of five, each with tests and its own PR. Status: ⬜ planned · 🟨 in progress · ✅ merged.

## Batch 1 — Habits & fun
1. ✅ **Forgot yesterday?** Log a deed for yesterday (still once per day).
2. ✅ **Habit heatmap** — 12-week grid per good/bad action.
3. ✅ **CSV export** of the whole ledger (download).
4. ✅ **Daily quests** — 3 quests a day from your good actions, +20 XP each.
5. ✅ **Level-up celebration** — banner when your rank or a dragon stage goes up.

## Batch 2 — Gold & dragons
6. ✅ **Gold coins** earned from deeds, quests and streaks.
7. ✅ **Treasure shop** — spend gold on dragon cosmetics.
8. ✅ **Dragon cosmetics** — hats, crowns, auras, colour variants.
9. ✅ **Favourite dragon** shown big on the home page.
10. ✅ **Weekly boss battle** — beat the boss with good deeds; bad habits heal it.

## Batch 3 — Finance (local only, no bank login)
11. ✅ **Monthly budget** by category (pence-accurate).
12. ✅ **Spending log** — manual entries.
13. ✅ **Savings goals** with progress.
14. ✅ **Bank CSV import** (download from your bank, import locally; duplicates skipped).
15. ✅ **Budget rewards** — Honour/XP for staying under budget each month.

## Batch 4 — Body
16. ⬜ **Workout log** — exercises, sets, reps, weight; personal bests.
17. ⬜ **Running log** — distance, time, pace; PBs.
18. ⬜ **Water tracker** — daily glasses.
19. ⬜ **Sleep log** — bed/wake time, hours.
20. ⬜ **Body weight** trend.

## Batch 5 — Mind
21. ⬜ **Reading tracker** — books, pages, finish dates.
22. ⬜ **Security+ flashcards** with spaced repetition.
23. ⬜ **Journal** — a short daily entry.
24. ⬜ **Mood check-in** — 1–5 with trend.
25. ⬜ **Focus timer** (Pomodoro) that logs study time.

## Batch 6 — Polish
26. ⬜ **Weekly report** page — wins, misses, streaks, dragons.
27. ⬜ **Search & filter** actions and history.
28. ⬜ **Reorder actions** (move up/down).
29. ⬜ **Offline app shell** (PWA service worker) for iPhone/PC.
30. ⬜ **Home dashboard** — today's quests, streaks, favourite dragon, reminders.

Bigger items still separate: iPhone sync with the PC (device pairing + Tailscale), Open Banking connection.
